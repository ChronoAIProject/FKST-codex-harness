# Engagement styleguide (induced from successful issue threads)

Comment on an issue the way these moves succeeded on similar issues; obey these rules.

Induced from 339 successful of 615 threads. Rules:

- Lead with a concrete reproduction: repro steps plus version/OS.
- Confirm the diagnosis with evidence before proposing a change.
- Offer to implement the fix yourself.
- Ask the maintainers for direction before implementing.
- Answer maintainer info-requests promptly and specifically.
- Cite the root cause precisely as file:line.
- Keep the comment concise (around 177 words).
- Disclose that the analysis is AI-assisted.
